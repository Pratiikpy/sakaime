## What was measured

The FDA summary parser (`src/extraction/fda-summary.ts`) was run over the 12 summaries in `data/fixtures/feasibility/fda` (the `pdftotext -layout` text of the real FDA PDFs, pages split on form feeds) and scored against `tests/extraction/gold/fda-gold.json`.

- These 12 documents are the DEVELOPMENT set: the parser was changed while looking at them, so these scores are in-sample.
- Gold: labelled by ONE reader, who also wrote the extractor and is not blind to it. The labels were written from the documents; they have not been checked by a second reader. Treat the scores as a development measurement, not an independent one.
- A value counts as extracted only if it passed BOTH checks (quote-exists and semantic support). Tables are not parsed and are not in the gold (see the limits below).
- Precision is reported where the gold is meant to be exhaustive. For sample sizes and site counts the gold lists the principal counts only, so only recall is a score and the other counts are listed as `other`.
- Performance figures the gold did not name were read against their passage and judged (`tests/extraction/gold/fda-adjudication.json`); precision credits the ones judged correct.
- Only 1 of the 12 PDFs is committed (DEN180001); the pdfjs path is exercised on it in the tests. The text fixtures are the same documents run through `pdftotext`, so the scores here describe the parser on that text, not pdfjs on all 12 files (see the pdfjs section).

## Known limits, from reading the documents

- Results that exist only in tables are not extracted. EyeArt K200667 states its pivotal sensitivity and specificity only in a stacked-cell table (24 table rows with several values were seen and counted, none extracted). A table needs cell geometry and column headers; this parser reads sentences and single-value rows only.
- Text-layer damage defeats some sentences. DEN170073 and DEN200055 carry recognition errors ('Trnth', 'repo1ts', 'res ectivel y', 'emolled'); a quote that reproduces the damaged characters still matches, but a sentence broken inside a number or a metric word is not read. In DEN200080, 14 of 25 PDF pages have no text layer at all (scans) and nothing is read from them.
- A lost comparison glyph hides the direction of a limit. The pdftotext text of K232699 reads 'age  18' (the greater-or-equal sign is missing), so no age limit is extracted from that text and the parser does not guess a direction; pdfjs keeps the glyph and reads the limit from the PDF. K250680's form page loses the sign too, and its summary page keeps it.
- Comparison tables that set the proposed and predicate devices side by side are flagged `comparison_table`; figures on those pages are UNCHECKED, not used.
- No fixture states that clinical testing was not conducted, so that rule is covered by a unit test on a constructed sentence only.
- Conditions, excluded groups and the diagnostic-use exclusions of the indications are read by the templates in `src/extraction/population.ts` (each names the text it was written from). A condition worded in a way no template covers is not extracted and stays absent: 'foci that are suspicious for cancer' (DEN200080) is the miss in the development gold. A sentence that excludes diagnostic use outside the indications block (DEN200055, 'not intended to be used as a stand-alone diagnostic device') is not read.
- The study's care setting is read only from a sentence that places the study (enrolled, sites, centers, conducted, took place, setting). A study that does not say where it took place has no setting, and the compiler then cannot tie its results to a claimed setting.
- The new fields (population conditions, excluded groups, diagnostic-use exclusions, study settings) were labelled in the gold after the templates had been written from the same 12 documents. Their scores are in-sample and say what the templates miss in documents they were written from, not how they do elsewhere.
