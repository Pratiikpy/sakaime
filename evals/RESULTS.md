# Evaluation results

Generated by `npx tsx evals/report.ts` from the files in `evals/results/`; do not edit by hand. Protocol: `docs/06-evaluation-protocol.md`. Every number is a link to the row of a results file it comes from. Re-run everything with `npx tsx evals/run-all.ts`.

## What these numbers are, and are not

- **consistency** results compare the implementation with a rule table or a specification the same builders wrote. They show the code does what the table says. They do not show the table is clinically right.
- **builder-labelled** results are scored against labels one builder wrote from the source documents (a second pass is a mechanical oracle written by the same person). No clinician and no independent reviewer has read any key. **No sentence here claims clinical or independent validation.**
- **domain-validity** results would need qualified, blind reviewers. There are none, so this report contains none.
- Demand is not measured here at all. Cost and latency figures are not reported except where stated.
- Every claim in the sets is CONSTRUCTED (written for the test in the style of a vendor claim); none is a statement by a vendor.
- Development and held-out results are never pooled. Cases of one device are correlated: intervals marked *cluster* come from a seeded bootstrap that resamples whole devices, and the effective sample size is the number of devices, not of cases.

**One rule accounts for every miss.** `date-past-window` (12 cases) expects REQUIRES_DOMAIN_REVIEW when a claim begins long after the clearance and the hospital policy sets a three-year window. The compiler applies its staleness rule (R20) to dated *study* evidence only; an authorization record is not aged and the FDA summaries yield no study date, so for authorization-only evidence the rule never fires. With a dated study the same mutation matches (`date-past-window-dated-study`). This is a design decision to confirm, not a harness error: it is why the held-out claim HO-CA-04 is expected to be missed.

All 12 false-supported cases in this table are the `date-past-window` misses: the compiler answered SUPPORTED_FOR_SCOPE where the rule table says a reviewer must decide.

The identity-ambiguity mutation of the protocol is not a compiler input; it is covered by IdentityShift, and the product-level mutations here (`product-replaced`, `product-brand-only`) test only that a product the evidence does not name is not supported.

## ScopeShift (B): authentic claims (builder-labelled)

Claims are CONSTRUCTED from real FDA summaries, registry records and publications; labels are builder-labelled (single builder, two passes, not independent). Model: `nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning-30b-a3b` through NVIDIA's API at temperature 0; replies are recorded under `results/model-runs` and replayed on re-run. Access differences between arms: B1 to B4 receive the FDA summary plus **every** candidate study or publication that the labelled identity corpus pairs with the device, including records of other products with the same brand (those candidates are the real collisions the identity-corpus builder selected, not the output of a live search, so the share of wrong candidates is higher than a random search would give); V receives the same documents through the identity layer, which keeps only the candidates it links. B3 and B4 go through a harness client without the product's 32 KB prompt rule (the records are public). B5 (Zetaris natural-language-to-SQL) was **not run**: Zetaris natural-language-to-SQL: BLOCKED. No natural-language-to-SQL service exists in the local build (infra/zetaris/GATES.md, gate Z-NL: three ways of finding one are written down there). B0 (manual research) belongs to the reviewer study and was not run. B6: the Constat and HealthIT arms emit no verdict; their scored results are reused from `evals/gate0`: a reviewer could re-derive the key from their output for [7 of 26 (Constat) and 0 of 26 (HealthIT)](results/scopeshift-b-dev.json#dev.gate0Rule) development claims. #### Sensitivity to the post-hoc edit of the key (ERRATA E-001)

The edit widened the acceptable set of CP-01 to include CONTRADICTED_BY_SPECIFIED_EVIDENCE. Only an arm that answered CONTRADICTED_BY_SPECIFIED_EVIDENCE on CP-01 gains acceptable credit from it; the exact label is unaffected. CP-01 answers: V: INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE; V-M1: CONTRADICTED_BY_SPECIFIED_EVIDENCE; V-noident: INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE; B1: INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE; B2: INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE; B3 run 1: CONTRADICTED_BY_SPECIFIED_EVIDENCE; B3 run 2: CONTRADICTED_BY_SPECIFIED_EVIDENCE; B3 run 3: CONTRADICTED_BY_SPECIFIED_EVIDENCE; B4: INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. The hand-written evidence items (V-M1 only) were also re-encoded after the dry run; V and every other arm do not use them.

#### Why V answers INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE so often (development set)

Dimensions the finding lists as not contained, over the 26 cases (a case can list several): population:DISJOINT 14, setting:UNKNOWN 14, population:UNKNOWN 12, version:UNKNOWN 6, purpose:DISJOINT 2, purpose:UNKNOWN 2, version:DISJOINT 1, product:UNKNOWN 1.

The extraction layer reads age, setting, purpose, version, dates and headline results from an FDA summary. It does not read the *conditions* a population is defined by or the *exclusions*, and it gives a study item neither a setting nor a purpose, while the compiler compares claimed conditions and exclusions as exact strings and, for a claim that rests on a study, needs the study to state the setting. So a claim that names a condition ("women undergoing screening mammography") cannot be contained in extracted evidence. The compiler refuses with the missing dimension named, which is the cautious direction: V made no false-supported error on the development set. The cost is that V confirms none of the nine positive controls from extracted evidence. Closing this gap is extraction work (conditions, exclusions, study setting), not compiler work; the hand-written evidence (V-M1) shows what the compiler does when it is closed.

## Ablations that can be measured

- **Compiler off (B3 and B4 against V).** The generic Nemotron arms have no compiler and no identity layer and read the same documents: B3 [mean 44.9% exact (worst 42.3%)](results/scopeshift-b-dev.json#dev.B3.exact.meanWorst) with 7/17, 6/17, 7/17 false-supported across 3 runs; B4 [mean 46.2% exact (worst 46.2%)](results/scopeshift-b-dev.json#dev.B4.exact.meanWorst) with 5/17 false-supported across 1 run; V 30.8% exact with 0 false-supported. Both generic arms are more often exactly right than V, and both make false-supported errors that V does not. V's advantage on this set is that it does not over-claim, not exact-label accuracy; the paired table gives the size of each difference and its uncertainty.
- **Compiler on complete evidence (V-M1 against B3/B4).** [61.5%](results/scopeshift-b-dev.json#dev.V-M1.exact) exact with [0/17](results/scopeshift-b-dev.json#dev.V-M1.falseSupported) false-supported. The hand-written items encode the key's own vocabulary, so this is an upper bound on what the compiler can do with the key's evidence, not a product result.
- **Identity layer off (V-noident against V).** Exact label [30.8%](results/scopeshift-b-dev.json#dev.V-noident.exact) against [30.8%](results/scopeshift-b-dev.json#dev.V.exact): no difference in labels, because V is constant (INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE) either way. The identity layer's own effect is measured in IdentityShift (false merges [0/60](results/identityshift.json#resolver.falseMergeRate) against the fuzzy-name baseline's [23/60](results/identityshift.json#fuzzyNameBaseline.falseMergeRate)). Critical-mismatch recall differs: V [45.0% (9/20)](results/scopeshift-b-dev.json#dev.V.criticalMismatchRecall) against V-noident [65.0% (13/20)](results/scopeshift-b-dev.json#dev.V-noident.criticalMismatchRecall).
- **What the identity layer changes before the compiler is reached.** The labelled corpus pairs the development devices with 53 candidate studies and publications (21 the builder labelled true links, 21 non-matches such as another module of the same brand, 11 ambiguous). With the layer off all 53 are attached to the product. With it on, 0 of the 21 non-matches and 0 of the 11 ambiguous records reach the compiler, and 10 of the 21 true links do (of the rest, 9 were left AMBIGUOUS, which shows them to the reviewer, and 2 were not proposed or were refused). Candidate verdicts are in the `retrieval` section of the file; builder labels are used here only to score them.
- **Held-out, compiler off.** V [30.0% (6/20)](results/scopeshift-b-held-out.json#heldout.V.exact) exact with [0.0% (0/15)](results/scopeshift-b-held-out.json#heldout.V.falseSupported) Wilson 0.0% to 20.4%; zero observed, exact 95% upper bound 18.1% false-supported; B3 [mean 63.3% exact (worst 60.0%)](results/scopeshift-b-held-out.json#heldout.B3.exact.meanWorst) with 4/15, 3/15, 3/15 false-supported across its runs; B4 [60.0% exact](results/scopeshift-b-held-out.json#heldout.B4.exact.meanWorst) with 3/15 false-supported (one run). On the version traps the generic arms follow the string: HO-PH-04 (the predicate's version) and HO-SD-02 (the predicate's model number) were answered SUPPORTED_FOR_SCOPE by B3 in every run and by B4. On this set the generic arms are more often exactly right than V and V is the only arm family with no false-supported answer; with 5 devices neither statement has a tight interval.
- Not measured: federation off (Zetaris stopped is a FailureShift drill, not an accuracy ablation), each Meterless engine (their ablations are in `tests/engines/ABLATIONS.md`, `tests/engines/scout/RESULTS.md`, `tests/engines/markovian/MA3-RESULTS.md`), the critic role (not built), and Nightshift receipts (MA-5).

## Defects and findings for the lead

1. **CompilerInvariantError on valid input.** `compile` throws "the narrower scope derived for population is itself PARTIAL" for the Gate 0 claim AN-02 on the hand-written evidence: the claim lists the condition "paced rhythm", which the study item also lists as an exclusion. A refusal or a label was expected, not an exception. In the harness an exception is a no-verdict answer and counts against the arm.
2. **Extraction to compiler hand-off** (above): conditions, exclusions and the study's setting and purpose are not carried, so claims that name them are refused. The compiler also compares conditions as exact strings, so even complete extraction needs a controlled condition vocabulary.
3. **Staleness is not applied to authorization records** (ScopeShift A). The held-out claim HO-CA-04 (a use beginning four years after clearance, hospital window three years) is labelled REQUIRES_DOMAIN_REVIEW; V answered INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE with timing UNKNOWN ("the study records carry no date"), because FDA-sourced study items have no date and the staleness rule reads only study dates.
4. **Extraction reports a subgroup figure as a second headline** for K253801 (sensitivity 0.806 beside 0.789): two values for one metric reach the study item, and a threshold claim is then judged against more than one number.
5. **Purpose collapse.** An FDA summary that says both detection and triage is read as the broader purpose (detection aid) once the purpose terms are mapped, so a triage-only claim is contained and a claim of diagnosis is a narrower scope; the sentence "not intended to be used as a diagnostic device" is not a rule. On HO-CA-03 and HO-EF-03 the compiler recorded purpose PARTIAL ("diagnosis outside detection aid"), i.e. a narrower scope; the final label was INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE only because the unextracted condition dimension came first. With conditions extracted, these two claims would be reported as a narrower scope where the key says CONTRADICTED_BY_SPECIFIED_EVIDENCE.
