# Evaluation results

Generated by `npx tsx evals/report.ts` from the files in `evals/results/`; do not edit by hand. Protocol: `docs/06-evaluation-protocol.md`. Every number is a link to the row of a results file it comes from. Re-run everything with `npx tsx evals/run-all.ts`.

## What these numbers are, and are not

- **consistency** results compare the implementation with a rule table or a specification the same builders wrote. They show the code does what the table says. They do not show the table is clinically right.
- **builder-labelled** results are scored against labels one builder wrote from the source documents (a second pass is a mechanical oracle written by the same person). No clinician and no independent reviewer has read any key. **No sentence here claims clinical or independent validation.**
- **domain-validity** results would need qualified, blind reviewers. There are none, so this report contains none.
- Demand is not measured here at all. Cost and latency figures are not reported except where stated.
- Every claim in the sets is CONSTRUCTED (written for the test in the style of a vendor claim); none is a statement by a vendor.
- Development and held-out results are never pooled. Cases of one device are correlated: intervals marked *cluster* come from a seeded bootstrap that resamples whole devices, and the effective sample size is the number of devices, not of cases.

#### Why V answers INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE so often (development set)

Dimensions the finding lists as not contained, over the 26 cases (a case can list several): population:UNKNOWN 22, setting:UNKNOWN 8, version:UNKNOWN 6, population:DISJOINT 4, purpose:DISJOINT 2, purpose:UNKNOWN 2, version:DISJOINT 1, product:UNKNOWN 1, setting:PARTIAL 1.

The extraction layer reads age, setting, purpose, version, dates and headline results from an FDA summary, and (adapter version 2, ERRATA E-005) the conditions and exclusions of the indications and a study setting where the summary states one. The compiler places a claimed condition against the evidence's wording only by its closed rules (normal form, a short curated synonym and subtype table, a closed list of narrowing words); a pair it cannot place is not guessed, and for a claim that rests on a study the study record has to state the population and the setting the results were measured in. Why the dimensions are open, from the compiler's own reasons: population (26): 8 x a claimed condition's wording cannot be placed against the evidence's wording; 6 x the study records state no population, so the measured results cannot be tied to the claimed one; 5 x other; 4 x the claim and the evidence name different values; 2 x no record in use states a population; 1 x the claim leaves out a condition or an age range the evidence states. purpose (4): 2 x the claim and the evidence name different values; 2 x other. setting (9): 6 x the study records state no care setting; 2 x the claimed setting is not in the taxonomy; 1 x the claim asks for more than the evidence covers. version (7): 6 x no record in use states a software version; 1 x the claim and the evidence name different values. product (1): 1 x other. The compiler refuses with the missing dimension named, which is the cautious direction: V made 0 false-supported errors on the development set. V confirms 0 of the 9 positive controls from extracted evidence. The hand-written evidence (V-M1) shows what the compiler does when the evidence states every dimension in the key's own vocabulary.

## Ablations that can be measured

- **Compiler off (B3 and B4 against V).** The generic Nemotron arms have no compiler and no identity layer and read the same documents: B3 [mean 44.9% exact (worst 42.3%)](results/scopeshift-b-dev.json#dev.B3.exact.meanWorst) with 7/17, 6/17, 7/17 false-supported across 3 runs; B4 [mean 46.2% exact (worst 46.2%)](results/scopeshift-b-dev.json#dev.B4.exact.meanWorst) with 5/17 false-supported across 1 run; V 34.6% exact with 0 false-supported. Both generic arms are more often exactly right than V, and both make false-supported errors that V does not. V's advantage on this set is that it does not over-claim, not exact-label accuracy; the paired table gives the size of each difference and its uncertainty.
- **Compiler on complete evidence (V-M1 against B3/B4).** [61.5%](results/scopeshift-b-dev.json#dev.V-M1.exact) exact with [0/17](results/scopeshift-b-dev.json#dev.V-M1.falseSupported) false-supported. The hand-written items encode the key's own vocabulary, so this is an upper bound on what the compiler can do with the key's evidence, not a product result.
- **Identity layer off (V-noident against V).** Exact label [34.6%](results/scopeshift-b-dev.json#dev.V-noident.exact) against [34.6%](results/scopeshift-b-dev.json#dev.V.exact): the same number of exact labels. The identity layer's own effect is measured in IdentityShift (false merges [0/60](results/identityshift.json#resolver.falseMergeRate) against the fuzzy-name baseline's [23/60](results/identityshift.json#fuzzyNameBaseline.falseMergeRate)). Critical-mismatch recall differs: V [65.0% (13/20)](results/scopeshift-b-dev.json#dev.V.criticalMismatchRecall) against V-noident [70.0% (14/20)](results/scopeshift-b-dev.json#dev.V-noident.criticalMismatchRecall).
- **What the identity layer changes before the compiler is reached.** The labelled corpus pairs the development devices with 53 candidate studies and publications (21 the builder labelled true links, 21 non-matches such as another module of the same brand, 11 ambiguous). With the layer off all 53 are attached to the product. With it on, 0 of the 21 non-matches and 0 of the 11 ambiguous records reach the compiler, and 10 of the 21 true links do (of the rest, 9 were left AMBIGUOUS, which shows them to the reviewer, and 2 were not proposed or were refused). Candidate verdicts are in the `retrieval` section of the file; builder labels are used here only to score them.
- **Held-out, compiler off.** V [45.0% (9/20)](results/scopeshift-b-held-out.json#heldout.V.exact) exact with [0.0% (0/15)](results/scopeshift-b-held-out.json#heldout.V.falseSupported) Wilson 0.0% to 20.4%; zero observed, exact 95% upper bound 18.1% false-supported; B3 [mean 63.3% exact (worst 60.0%)](results/scopeshift-b-held-out.json#heldout.B3.exact.meanWorst) with 4/15, 3/15, 3/15 false-supported across its runs; B4 [60.0% exact](results/scopeshift-b-held-out.json#heldout.B4.exact.meanWorst) with 3/15 false-supported (one run). On the version traps the generic arms follow the string: HO-PH-04 (the predicate's version) and HO-SD-02 (the predicate's model number) were answered SUPPORTED_FOR_SCOPE by B3 in every run and by B4. On this set the generic arms are more often exactly right than V and V is the only arm family with no false-supported answer; with 5 devices neither statement has a tight interval.
- Not measured: federation off (Zetaris stopped is a FailureShift drill, not an accuracy ablation), each Meterless engine (their ablations are in `tests/engines/ABLATIONS.md`, `tests/engines/scout/RESULTS.md`, `tests/engines/markovian/MA3-RESULTS.md`), the critic role (not built), and Nightshift receipts (MA-5).
